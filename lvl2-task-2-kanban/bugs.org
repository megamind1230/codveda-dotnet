** Open

*** BUG 28 — HTTPS redirect warning (all 4 projects)

- Log: "Failed to determine the https port for redirect."
- Cause: app.UseHttpsRedirection() is called in every Program.cs but no HTTPS
  port is configured in launchSettings.json.
- Affects: Kanban.Mvc, Kanban.RazorPages, Kanban.Mixed.Minimal,
  Kanban.Mixed.ApiController — all 4 projects.
- Fix: Disable UseHttpsRedirection() or configure an HTTPS URL in
  launchSettings.json. Cosmetic only — app works over HTTP on localhost.
